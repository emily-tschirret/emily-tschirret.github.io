Fowlerville High School Chooses Spring Play!

Director Mrs. Martin Says This will be the best spring play ever!!

Mrs. Martin, coming off a hot streak with School of Rock last year, has offically chosen The Play That Goes Wrong for this year's play! This play is very tech-heavy, which will give our technicians plenty of time to work and really design something they can be proud of.

Her and her technicians plan to take the lessons learned from last year's School of Rock and apply them here, including but not limited to the importance of set build and how to hang various things on various battens.

The crew is year is made up of a lot of veterans, with a lot of young talent bringing up the rear to ensure a smooth transition once our seniors graduate. Our starting lineup is:

Emily at Lighting Director (Returning for 5th year)
Sienna at Sound Director (Returning for 3rd year)
Lydia at Rigging Director (New at this position)
Camden at Stage Director (New at this position)

And their respective crews working under them.

We got a statement from Mrs. Martin on any worries she may have on the production of the play. She said:

"I believe any issues that may arise our tech crew will be able to tacke them using lessons learned in the years before. I have nothing but the utmost confidence in this production.
--Quote from Jill Martin, ACPA Director and Manager
